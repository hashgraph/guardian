This roadmap is a living document, organized into three categories: **Now** — well-defined items ready to be delivered; **Next** — items with some open questions still to resolve; and **Later** — not yet prioritized and requiring further validation. Development is visible and active — track progress directly via [GitHub milestones](https://github.com/hashgraph/guardian/milestones?sort=due_date&direction=asc).

{% hint style="warning" %}
This roadmap may change, and this page is updated only intermittently — it may not reflect the most recent product direction. For the latest, follow active development on GitHub, send us your ideas by email, or subscribe to the community calendar (see below).
{% endhint %}
