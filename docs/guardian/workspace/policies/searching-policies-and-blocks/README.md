# How to search policies and policy blocks?

Guardian lets policy authors search for similar content across every published policy in the instance, directly from the search button in the policy editor.

* **Search a policy** to find other policies with a similar overall workflow. Results show a similarity percentage against your source policy.
* **Search a block** to find other occurrences of that block in a similar context — i.e. embedded in policy content with a similar surrounding structure (the same neighboring blocks) — across all published policies.

See the following pages for step-by-step instructions:
