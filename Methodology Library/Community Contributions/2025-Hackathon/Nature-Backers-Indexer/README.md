# Nature Backers Indexer

### Hedera Hello Future Apex Hackathon 2026 | Sustainability Track

Nature Backers is a sustainability engagement application developed by Nature Wired for the Apex Hackathon.

The submission demonstrates how the **Hedera Guardian Global Indexer** can support a downstream sponsor and fan engagement experience. Sponsors discover and curate sustainability projects through the Indexer, present selected projects to fans during a live sporting event, and use Hedera to record participation and campaign results.

> **Guardian contribution note:** Nature Backers did not create a new Guardian policy or methodology during the hackathon. This contribution is an example of using the Guardian Global Indexer for project discovery and curation.

## How It Works

**Guardian Global Indexer**  
→ discover and curate sustainability projects  
→ sponsor pre-selects projects before the event  
→ fans explore and back one project during the event  
→ participation is recorded using Hedera Consensus Service  
→ sponsor funding is allocated based on campaign results  
→ optional digital participation rewards are issued using Hedera Token Service

## Apex Use Case

The hackathon scenario demonstrates a women’s flag football activation in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Before game time, a sponsor pre-selects three sustainability projects based on factors such as methodology, SDG alignment and geography. During the event, fans scan a QR code, learn about the projects, back one project and see the collective results.

## Why This Example Is Useful

Nature Backers demonstrates how Guardian-indexed project information can be used beyond methodology and registry workflows in a user-facing engagement application.

Although the Apex example focuses on women’s sports, the same pattern could be applied to other sporting events, corporate campaigns, employee engagement or community participation.

## Hackathon Learnings

The Apex work demonstrated the value of the Guardian Global Indexer for discovering and filtering sustainability projects by factors such as SDG alignment, geography, methodology and verification.

It also highlighted the additional work required to turn Guardian-indexed data into a simple, responsive experience for non-technical users. Nature Backers used normalization, filtering, caching and pre-curation to make project information easier for sponsors and fans to explore during a live event.

These learnings provided input into later discussions around the **Sustainability Atlas** and the opportunity for a broader project discovery and exploration layer across Guardian-indexed projects.

Nature Backers complements that direction by exploring how selected projects can then be used in sponsor campaigns, fan participation and auditable engagement.
